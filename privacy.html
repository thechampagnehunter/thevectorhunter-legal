Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "The Vector Hunter" ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its TikTok integration.

1. What we do

The Vector Hunter is an automated content publishing tool. It generates and posts short videos summarizing trading results from a crypto trading bot, using data retrieved from the Bitget API. Posting to TikTok is done via the TikTok Content Posting API, using an account authorized by the app owner.

2. Information we collect

We collect and process the following information:

  • TikTok account authorization tokens (access token / refresh token) obtained via TikTok's official OAuth process, used solely to publish content to the authorized account.
  • Basic public profile information associated with the authorized TikTok account (such as username), used only to confirm successful authentication.
  • Trading performance data (prices, profit/loss percentages, leverage) retrieved from the Bitget API, used to generate video content. This data does not include personal information about any third party.

We do not collect personal information from TikTok viewers or from any user other than the authorized account owner.

3. How we use information

Information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate and publish video content to the authorized TikTok account.
  • Generate accurate trading result summaries for video content.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, except as required to operate the integration (e.g., communication with TikTok's and Bitget's own APIs).

4. Data storage and security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored securely on a private server and are not exposed publicly. Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect this data from unauthorized access.

5. Data retention

Authorization tokens are retained only for as long as necessary to operate the integration, and are deleted if the TikTok account is disconnected or authorization is revoked.

6. Your rights

The account owner may revoke the app's access to their TikTok account at any time via TikTok's account settings. Upon revocation, the app will no longer be able to access or publish to that account.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app owner through the contact information provided on the associated GitHub repository.
